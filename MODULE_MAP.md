# ConsERP Module Map

## Core Platform
* **Tenancy & Auth:** Organisation management, User management, RBAC, Authentication.
* **SaaS Billing:** Subscriptions, Plan Management, Platform Invoicing.
* **Settings:** Organisation-level configurations (currency, tax rates, financial year).

## Operations
* **CRM & Tendering:** Leads, Opportunities, Quotations, Tenders.
* **QS & Estimating:** Cost Codes, Rate Libraries, BOQs, Estimates.
* **Project Management:** Projects, Work Packages, Milestones, Site Diary.
* **Manufacturing (Prefab):** BOMs, Production Orders, Material Issues.

## Supply Chain
* **Procurement:** Requisitions, RFQs, Purchase Orders, Goods Receipts.
* **Inventory:** Warehouses, Items, Stock Movements, Ledger.
* **Plant & Fleet:** Equipment, Maintenance, Fuel Usage, Timesheets.

## Human Resources & Compliance
* **HR & Payroll:** Employees, Contracts, Timesheets, Payroll Runs, Payslips.
* **SHEQ:** Incidents, Risk Assessments, Inspections, Corrective Actions.

## Financials
* **Job Costing:** Real-time project profitability and budget vs actuals.
* **Double-Entry Accounting:** GL, AP, AR, Cashbook, Journals, Fixed Assets.
* **Subcontractors:** Valuations, Payment Certificates, Retentions.

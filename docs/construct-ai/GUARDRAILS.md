# ConstructAI Guardrails & Risk Levels

## Risk Classification
- **Level 0 (Informational):** Search, summarise, explain (executed immediately).
- **Level 1 (Draft):** Draft requisitions, site diaries, reports (creates drafts).
- **Level 2 (Operational):** Submit requisitions, stock transfers (requires confirmation).
- **Level 3 (Financial / Sensitive):** Post journals, approve POs, payroll (requires human approval).
- **Level 4 (Prohibited):** Bypass audit logs, alter posted ledger history, grant permissions (strictly blocked).

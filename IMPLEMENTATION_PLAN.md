# ConsERP Implementation Plan

## PHASE 1: Foundation
* Authentication, Multi-Tenancy (Organisations, Organisation Switching).
* SaaS Subscription Architecture.
* Role-Based Access Control (RBAC).
* Platform Super Admin & Tenant Admin setup.
* Audit Logging foundation.
* Functional React Interface.

## PHASE 2: Master Data
* Organisation setup (Branches, Departments).
* Customers and Suppliers.

## PHASE 3: CRM & Estimating
* Leads, Prospects, Quotations, Tendering.

## PHASE 4: QS & Cost Control
* BOQs, Cost Codes, Rate Build-ups.

## PHASE 5: Projects
* Projects, Contracts, WBS, Site Management.

## PHASE 6: Procurement
* Requisitions, Purchase Orders, Supplier Management.

## PHASE 7: Inventory
* Warehouses, Items, Stock Ledger, Movements.

## PHASE 8: Prefab Manufacturing
* Bill of Materials, Production Orders.

## PHASE 9: Plant & Fleet
* Equipment tracking, Maintenance.

## PHASE 10: HR & Payroll
* Employees, Timesheets, Payroll engine.

## PHASE 11: SHEQ
* Safety, Quality, and Environmental tracking.

## PHASE 12: Subcontractors & Progress
* Valuations, Variations, Certificates.

## PHASE 13: Financials
* GL, AP, AR, Double-Entry Accounting.

## PHASE 14: Job Costing
* Full integration of costs to projects.

## PHASE 15: Reporting & Dashboards
* Dashboards by role, Document Management.

## PHASE 16: Platform & Integrations
* SaaS Billing execution, Xero Connector prep.

## PHASE 17: Hardening
* Security, Performance, E2E Testing, Laravel Cloud readiness.
